# Enemies and traps

Every hunter has one job that changes the route Sally takes, rather than just being
dodged. Wherever an attack commits, it aims where Sally is heading, so walking
straight on is rarely enough: change direction, break line of sight, or hide.

## Crawling
Hold **C** (or the Crawl button on phones) to crawl: about half walking pace, no sprint, and never
any noise, even on gravel. It is a slight edge, not a disguise:

- Snakes, sea snakes and cobras on watch spot a crawling Sally about a tile closer, and take
  about 1.2 s instead of 0.7 s to be sure before they chase.
- A snow fox hears a crawl only from about a tile away (2.6 walking); a mole about 2 tiles (4.5).
- Crickets, scorpions and wasp nests react to noise, and a crawl makes none.
- A mimic springs at 1.2 tiles instead of 1.7; a gecko notices from 3.5 tiles instead of 6.
- A flytrap snaps 0.55 s after a crawling Sally enters its dew-marked reach, instead of 0.32 s.
- Everything else (mantis, heron, crab, eel, a cobra's strike, owl, hawk, bat, dragonfly, toad,
  spider, beetle, fish, angler) is unchanged: crawling into a strike still gets Sally caught.

## Throwing
Hold **Q** (a pebble) or **L** (a glow mushroom), or hold the button on phones, to throw further:
a tap goes about 2.6 tiles, a full 0.75 s hold about 6. While held, the arrows or stick aim
(diagonals too) and Sally stands still; a dotted line shows the flight. Throws bank off walls up
to twice, losing a quarter of their reach each time, and fly over gaps and water.

- A **pebble** clacks where it hits a wall, so a hunter hears it there first, then where it lands.
- A **glow mushroom** lights the ground 2.5 tiles round where it lands for about 12 s, even round
  a corner from Sally. Standing in its light lights Sally up too: leaves stop hiding her.
- Fogged mazes from escape 20 on carry one; the darkest and biggest carry two.

## Tricks of the ground

- **Moss** (`m` on a map): every step is silent there, even a sprint, and moles feel nothing.
- **Echo halls** (`echoes`, rectangles): any sound made inside carries nearly twice as far,
  Sally's steps and a pebble's clack alike.
- **Currents** (`currents`: an area, a direction and a strength): water in the area pushes a
  swimmer along. Sally swims about 1.3 tiles a second, so a current stronger than that cannot
  be swum against.
- **Burrow pairs** (`tunnels`: two mounds): standing on a mound, press E (Burrow) to dig in and
  come up out of its partner a moment later. No burrow charge is used; nothing can catch Sally
  underground or follow her.
- **Spore pouches** (`spores`, held throw with Z): the cloud hides Sally even on the move and
  blinds any hunter inside it, for about six seconds.

## Hunters

| Hunter | Job | What it does | How to beat it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Snake | Chaser | Patrols, spots Sally, then chases, searches and gives up. In a chase it aims where she is heading and cuts across loops; run rings round it and it reads the loop and leads further. Cut back sharply at speed and it slides on, then whips round and surges. Close behind her it feels her even facing away. Brushing the back half of a resting snake (paused, coiled or watching) wakes it; a moving tail slides away. | Break sight at a corner, use leaves, or throw a pebble. Never step on a still snake's tail. |
| King cobra | Striker and hunter | Waits coiled on its post, swaying to watch. On sight it rears (a short warning line), strikes along where Sally is heading, then chases faster than any snake. When it loses her it glides home and coils again. | Sidestep the moment it rears, then break its sight at once. Never pass in front of its post. |
| Beetle | Battering ram | Locks a lane toward where Sally is heading (shown as a striped lane), then charges up to three tiles past its aim. A charge flattens leaf cover and smashes cracked walls open for everyone. It is dazed for a moment after hitting a wall. | Step out of the lane; never stand at the end of a long corridor. Lure it into a cracked wall to open a shortcut. |
| Scorpion | Living trap | Guards a ring around itself (always shown). Its tail rises and falls on a rhythm; with the tail up, anything entering the ring is stung where it is heading. Noise keeps the tail up. A buried scorpion shows only a sand mound until Sally is close. | Cross the ring while the tail is down and change direction if it strikes. Leaves inside the ring still hide Sally. |
| Toad | Sniper | Fires its tongue down any clear straight line toward Sally, up to five tiles. | Break its line of sight behind pillars, walls or leaves. |
| Spider | Builder | Leaves lasting tripwire webs on its rounds (up to five). Touching one slows Sally and sends the spider rushing. Up close it spits a sticky shot. | Watch the floor, step around webs, or move through them only when the spider is far away. |
| Cricket | Alarm | Harmless itself. When it sees or hears Sally it chirps, and hunters in earshot that cannot see her come to the spot for two seconds. A hunter that can see her ignores the call, it chirps at most every eight seconds, and it never drowns out a pebble Sally threw. | Stay quiet and out of its sight, or let it chirp somewhere useless. |
| Moth | Lantern | Its glow lights Sally up: leaves stop hiding her and every hunter sees further. It swoops on glowing footprints. | Keep out of its light; time crossings while it drifts away. |
| Echo frog | Mimic of sounds | Harmless itself. Noise near it (steps on gravel, a sprint, a pebble's clack) fills its throat; two seconds later it croaks the sound back loudly from where it sits, and hunters in earshot go to look there. Then it rests about six seconds. Lives in the Mossy Well. | Keep quiet near it, or make a noise on purpose and send the hunters to the frog. |
| Chameleon | Unseen stalker | All but invisible while it keeps still; a shimmer and a rustle give it away when it moves. It patrols with long pauses and chases like a snake, a little slower. Lives in the Menagerie. | Watch for the shimmer, listen for the rustle, and remember where it stopped. |
| Frost sentinel | Gaze of ice | A crystal statue that turns its gaze through set directions (`looks`, `every`, `range`). The gaze glances off ice walls (`I` on a map) up to twice, so it sees round corners; stone stops it. Caught in it, Sally gets a 0.6 s glint of warning; still there, she is frozen for 1.6 s and the crack calls every hunter nearby. It never catches her itself. Lives in the Ice Cave. | Read where the gaze bounces; step out at the glint, or hide in leaves or spores. |
| Cloud ray | Moving cover | Glides high over everything along its patrol loop, ignoring walls. Harmless. Its shadow (`range`, default 2.1 tiles) hides Sally from the hawk and from every hunter, even moving. Lives in the Cloud Nest. | Keep pace under the shadow to cross open ground while the hawk hunts. |
| Mimic | Greed trap | Looks like a treasure chest, with a slow breath and an occasional blink. When Sally reaches for it, it snaps toward where she is heading. | Learn the tell, or throw a pebble to make it snap first. |
| Millipede | Lurker | Freezes while watched and follows when Sally looks away, a little slower than she walks. Out of her gaze it shows as a dim shape creeping after her (with a scrape when it starts); once she faces it, it is solid and still. | Keep it in view while you back off. |
| Owl | Searchlight | Turns between look-outs with a long narrow beam, glares, then swoops; the swoop bends toward Sally. | Hide in leaves or behind cover before it lands. |
| Hawk | Sky | A shadow follows Sally and dives on a timer. | Shelter under an awning, in leaves, or blend in with camouflage. |
| Gecko | Thief | Steals the last treasure Sally picked up and runs for its burrow. | Catch it before it gets there. |
| Angler | Bait | Hides under dark water and dangles a glowing lure that looks like a glow bug. Step close, or reach for it with the tongue, and it lunges at that spot, then sinks for a few seconds before the lure lights again. A pebble landing by the lure springs it too. | Learn the slow bob and the faint line. Walk around the lure, or spring it from afar and pass while it sinks. |
| Flytrap | Rooted biter | Stands on its stem with its reach always marked. Anything that comes within reach, or a pebble landing there, makes it snap where Sally is heading; then it chews for a couple of seconds and is harmless. | Feed it a pebble and pass while it chews, or keep out of its ring. |
| Bog heron | Rooted striker | Stands frozen in the reeds with the line in front of its beak marked on the ground. Anything that steps into that line, or a pebble landing there, is stabbed after a quarter-second tell; then it recovers for over a second. Some turn their heads between set directions every few seconds. It watches the surface, so it never sees Sally swimming. | Cross while it looks the other way or recovers from stabbing a pebble, or swim past. |
| Praying mantis | Ambusher | Looks like a faint twig until Sally is within a couple of tiles. Watches a short cone in front of it (always shown once revealed). Anything that moves into the cone, Sally, a pebble or another hunter, gets a quarter-second tell as the forelegs rise, then a snatch; it recovers for over a second. A snatched hunter reels back dazed and walks on. | Spot the kinked twig, cross behind it or while it recovers, or let it snatch a pebble or a hunter first. |
| Wasp nest | Trap you can use | A paper nest. Noise within a few tiles, a pebble landing by it, a touch or a charging beetle stirs it: a buzz, then a swarm pours out and chases the nearest creature within six tiles of the nest for four seconds. A hunter it reaches is stung and dazed; Sally is caught. It loses anyone in leaves or water, then drifts home and settles. | Walk past it, never run. If it stirs, sprint out of range or duck into leaves or water. Throw a pebble by the nest when a hunter is near to set the swarm on it. |
| Dragonfly | Timing | Darts round a fixed loop (always dotted on the ground) over water, gaps and walls, far faster than Sally, hovering for a blink at each corner. Leaves do not hide Sally and pebbles do not distract it. Swimming under the surface is safe. | Read the loop; cross just behind it. |
| Mole | Tremor hunter | Blind; hunts by footsteps. Walking within 4.5 tiles (6 on gravel, 8 at a sprint) sends it tunnelling toward the sound, shown as a ripple of soil; when it is under Sally a shaking mound gives a 0.3-second tell and it bursts up. Footsteps on leaves, swimming, standing still and bounce-cap flights make no tremble; if the trail goes cold for half a second it surfaces where it is, sniffs and digs back down. A pebble's thud draws it. | Stop as soon as the ripple heads your way, keep to leaves, or sprint clear (though a sprint carries further). |
| Echo bat | Sonar | Flies a loop sending out a sonar ring every couple of seconds. If a ring sweeps over Sally while she is moving (a wind gust counts), it swoops on where she is heading. A still Sally is invisible to it; leaves make no difference. | Freeze when a ring spreads toward you, or change direction the moment it swoops. |
| Eel | Pool lurker | Hides in a water tile with only its eyes showing. Walking the water's edge within 1.6 tiles (2.2 when swimming) makes it ripple for 0.3 seconds, then lunge where Sally is heading; it sulks for a moment after. When the tide leaves its tile as sand it lies buried and harmless. | Keep a tile back from its pool, swim wide of it, or pass at low tide. |
| Hermit crab | Lane guard | Sits in its shell like any other on the beach. When Sally comes close in plain sight it peeks out, then scuttles sideways along its lane to cut her off and pinch. It never leaves its lane. A pebble landing by it sends it back into its shell for a few seconds. | Cross its lane at a sprint, while it is tucked in, or hidden; or toss a pebble first. |
| Snow fox | Ear hunter | Hunts by ear, not by eye. Footsteps close by, a sprint or noisy ground further off, or a pebble, and it crouches for a blink and pounces on the sound, far and fast, with no warning mark. It sniffs about where it lands, then goes back to its rounds. | Stand still, keep to quiet ground and walk rather than run near it, or toss a pebble to send its pounce elsewhere. |
| Ants | Moving wall | A column marches a fixed loop and catches anything it walks over. | Cross in the gaps between groups. |

## Traps

Levels opt into each trap through their level data.

| Trap | Level data | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-use bridge | `once: true` on a bridge gate | Rises from its plate once; when it closes it is gone for good. |
| Crumbling bridge | `crumble: true` on a bridge gate | Holds for one crossing, then falls away behind Sally. |
| Crumbling floor | `c` in a text map, or `crumble: [[x, y]]` | Cracks under Sally and collapses into a gap once she leaves it. There is no way back. |
| Rolling log | `logs: [{from, to, period, offset, speed}]` | Rumbles as a warning, then sweeps a straight lane. It flattens Sally and knocks hunters down. |
| Tangle vine | `T` in a text map | Holds Sally still for about a second and a half. If a hunter sees her while she is held, she is caught (a spare tail tears her free instead). Then the vine withers for a few seconds and regrows. |
| Crack | `S` in a text map | A narrow split in a wall. Sally slips through; no hunter can follow or see into it, so it ends a chase. |
| Heavy plate | `heavy: true` on a timed gate | Ignores Sally. Only a big hunter's weight (a beetle on its rounds) presses it, opening the gate for its duration. Wait for the beetle, then sneak through. |
| Thorns | `thorns: [{tiles, period, up, offset}]` | Tremble, then rise on a rhythm. They only catch Sally while raised. |
| Spore puffball | `o` in a text map | Sally stepping on it, a hunter walking over it or a pebble landing by it bursts it into a spore cloud for about four and a half seconds. Inside the cloud Sally is hidden even while moving, and a hunter inside it cannot see. It regrows after ten seconds. |
| Bounce cap | `caps: [{at, to}]` | Stepping onto the cap flings Sally in a half-second arc to its landing tile, over gaps, water or walls. She cannot be caught in the air, and the flight makes no footsteps. The arc is marked. |
| Wind lane | `wind: [{area: [x1, y1, x2, y2], dir, period, on, offset}]` | Streaks always show the lane and its direction; they thicken and race just before a gust. While it gusts, Sally inside is shoved along the lane, out of cover, and counts as moving. |
| Lightning | `storm: {every, warn, first}` | Thunder rumbles and the sky darkens, then a flash: for a moment every hunter with a clear line sees Sally, even in leaves. Walls (and spore clouds) still hide her. |
| Tide flat | `t` in a text map, `tide: {period, high, offset}` | Water for the first `high` seconds of each cycle (Sally swims, land hunters keep off), sand for the rest. Waves lap at the flats just before the tide turns. |

## Text maps

Rebuilt routes are drawn as text, one character per tile:

```
# wall   . floor   L leaf cover   N noisy gravel   B cracked wall   M mud   ~ water
_ gap    c crumbling floor   T tangle vine   S crack   o spore puffball   t tide flat   @ start   K key   X exit   1-5 treasure of that value
```

Guards, gates and traps keep their own lists, with coordinates matching the map.
`scripts/route-solver.cjs solve <levelId> "<hints>"` records a winning run for
`tests/fixtures/escape-playthroughs.json`. Its hints are waypoints such as `g12,4`,
tools such as `burrow`, and waits such as `w2`.
