# Little Explorers trail notes

Two optional reading spots in each new adventure. Coordinates are zero-based text-map tiles. Walk onto a little paper sign to read it; the whole simulation waits, including hunters and steam timers. Close with touch, mouse, Enter/Space, controller A/B, Escape or Android Back. Leave the spot and return to read it again. No campaign page, star, treasure or unlock is awarded.

Notes are deliberately short (30 words maximum), use the same tool names as the HUD, and explain the nearby object. They are not required to finish. Existing opening hints remain available.
